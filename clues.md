## Fair Play Checklist

Answer these questions to ensure your mystery plays fair with readers:

- [ ] Do all clues point to the actual solution (not a different one)?
- [ ] Are clues present before the solution is revealed?
- [ ] Can an attentive reader solve this with available information?
- [ ] Is at least one clue planted in each act?
- [ ] Are clues visible but not too obvious?
- [ ] Do red herrings have believable explanations when revealed as false?
- [ ] Does the detective have access to all clues the reader has (or vice versa)?
- [ ] Are there 2-4 red herrings (not too many, not too few)?
- [ ] Do clues accumulate to make the solution increasingly clear?
- [ ] Is there a moment where reader could figure it out before the detective?

---

## Clue Distribution Guide

**Minimum clues for fair play**: 5-7 true clues pointing to solution

**Recommended ratio**: 2-3 true clues for every 1 red herring

**Placement timing**:
- First clue: Within first 25% of story
- Final crucial clue: No later than 85% of story
- Spread remaining clues throughout

**Obviousness mix**:
- 2-3 subtle clues (only attentive readers notice)
- 2-3 moderate clues (detective and readers find)
- 1-2 clear clues (obvious in retrospect)
